Interact Design Hub
📌 Project Overview

Interact Design Hub is a web-based platform designed to connect clients with professional interior designers and architects. The platform focuses on creativity, usability, and attention to detail, ensuring users feel confident they are in the right place to transform their spaces.

Designers can showcase their proflile, best projects, and achievements, while clients can explore designs, share requirements.

🎯 Key Objectives

Bridge the gap between clients and interior designers

Provide a visually appealing and user-friendly experience

Enable designers to showcase creativity and professional achievements
